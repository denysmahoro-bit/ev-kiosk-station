FAQ

Questions, answered.

Here are a few things drivers commonly want to know.

How do I start charging?

Open the Customer portal to register your vehicle, then ask a station seller to start your charging session.

How can I pay for charging?

Payments are requested and confirmed through the mobile-money flow supported by the charging station.

Which vehicles can use the service?

Sela Volts is designed for electric vehicles. Contact us to confirm compatibility for your vehicle before visiting.

How can I check station availability?

Contact our team on +250 789 662 677 or by email before travelling to a station.
